Our Journey

Indian Nomads in Ingolstadt e.V. was built on a simple feeling—the longing for home. What began as a small vision among friends has evolved into a landmark cultural event.

1893 Birth of Sarvajanik Ganeshotsav

Lokmanya Bal Gangadhar Tilak transformed Ganesh Chaturthi from a private home ritual into a powerful public festival in Pune. This step turned the celebration into a symbol of unity, cultural pride, and collective strength across society.

1893 Establishment of Shri Kasba Ganpati Mandal

Shri Kasba Ganpati Sarvajanik Ganesh Mandal was established in Pune, marking one of the earliest and most significant milestones in the history of public Ganeshotsav. As the revered Gramdaivat of the city, it played a foundational role in shaping the organized celebration of Ganesh Chaturthi. Its enduring traditions of devotion, discipline, and community participation laid the cultural and spiritual groundwork that continues to influence Ganeshotsav practices in India and across the world today.

Late 19th–20th Century Rise of Manache Ganpati

In Pune, the roots of public Ganeshotsav are deeply anchored in the tradition of the Manache Ganpati, the most revered and historically significant Ganpati mandals of the city. This distinguished group includes Shri Kasba Ganpati Sarvajanik Ganesh Mandal (the Gramdaivat of Pune), along with Tambdi Jogeshwari, Guruji Talim, Tulshibaug, and Kesariwada Ganpati.Collectively, these mandals form the enduring cultural and spiritual framework of Pune’s Ganeshotsav tradition. Their legacy has shaped the public celebration of the festival through values of discipline, devotion, and community unity, serving as a model that continues to inspire Ganeshotsav celebrations across India and around the world.

2025 First 5-Day Ganpati Celebration in Ingolstadt (इंगोलस्टाटचा राजा)

A historic milestone for the Indian community as Ingolstadt hosts its first-ever 5-day Ganpati celebration, proudly established as “इंगोलस्टाटचा राजा.” This marks a significant beginning of collective devotion, cultural unity, and shared traditions in Germany, bringing the community together in celebration and strengthening cultural roots far from home..

2026 इंगोलस्टाटचा राजा (Upcoming Edition)

Ingolstadt is all set to come alive once again with Ganeshotsav 2026—bigger, brighter, and more vibrant than ever before. With growing participation, deeper devotion, and an even stronger sense of togetherness, this year promises an unforgettable celebration filled with energy, culture, and tradition. As the community unites once again, Ganeshotsav in Ingolstadt continues to evolve into a powerful cultural legacy in Europe, celebrating faith with renewed spirit and enthusiasm.

Helpful Info

FAQs

Is there an entry fee?

No, entry is free for everyone! We welcome all cultures to celebrate with us.

How can I reach the venue?

The venue Exerzierhaus Klenzepark is located in a central and well-connected area of Ingolstadt, making it easy to reach for all visitors.If you are nearby, the venue is easily accessible by a pleasant walk through Klenzepark. For public transport users, the nearest bus stops are Stadttheater and Brückenkopf, both of which are within short walking distance from the venue.

what are the nearby parking options?

There are several convenient choices near the location, including:

  1. Tiefgarage Reduit Tilly (P4)
  2. Parkplatz Theater Ost (P1)
  3. Congressgarage
  4. Theater Bus Parkplatz
  5. Tiefgarage Schloss (P2)
  6. Tiefgarage “Am Theater”
Can we book Aarti during the event? Is there a fee involved?

Yes, devotees can book the daily Aarti at Exerzierhaus Klenzepark in Ingolstadt.To support the arrangements, decorations, prasad, and overall event expenses, a small Aarti contribution/booking fee is requested for reserved participation. The devotee who books the slot will have the opportunity to perform the Aarti themselves, making it a personal and meaningful spiritual experience.Aarti slots are limited and will be allocated on a first-come, first-served basis or prior booking, depending on availability. We encourage devotees to reserve in advance for their preferred date.
